How to read the margin. Margin = dealer quote − (basic cost + COE). Basic cost is LTA’s published figure and uses the average OMV of cars registered that month, not your exact car. It excludes dealer commission, insurance, plates, road tax and all dealer overheads, so the margin is an upper bound on gross margin, not profit. Dealer quote should be the on-the-road price including COE, before insurance and loan.
